Addresses for the players

In the game press F6, write one of these in Server address and in Port, then Host to open a room; your friends pick it from the room list next to the panel.

Friends on the Internet need the port to be open on your router: automatic below, or by hand in the router settings (forward TCP port to this PC). With Tailscale, ZeroTier, Radmin VPN or Hamachi use that network's address instead: nothing to open.

Router port (UPnP)

Asks your router to forward TCP port to this PC while the app is open. Closed again when you quit.
